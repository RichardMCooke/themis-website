What nobody says out loud

Most founders don't actually want a CFO. They want someone else to carry the weight of the numbers they're carrying alone at 11pm.

A fractional CFO isn't billing you to reconcile the bank account. They do the high-impact work — the model, the forecast, the “should we actually do this deal” call.

Most businesses don't need a full-time CFO. They need CFO-level thinking on the decisions that matter.
