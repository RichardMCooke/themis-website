What we do

Five mandates, one operating discipline.

Our directors and consultants engage fractionally at two to eight days a month, or on a defined transformation mandate with a start, a scope and an end.

01 Fractional CFO 02 Growth & margin 03 Exit & M&A 04 Governance / NED 05 Data & BI How engagements work
01

Fractional CFO & Finance Director

The finance leadership a £10m–£100m business needs, on the days it needs it — resourced from our own team, not sub-contracted.

  • Systems, controls and reporting professionalised — ERP, BI and month-end that stand up to scrutiny
  • Forecasting accuracy, cash generation and working capital discipline
  • Board reporting and investor packs a buyer or lender will trust
  • Finance team structure, capability and succession
02

Growth, margin & cost-to-serve

Margin is rarely lost where people look for it.

  • Full structural cost reviews across manufacturing, supply chain and overheads
  • Pricing strategy, customer profitability and cost-to-serve by line
  • Operational productivity, OEE and labour efficiency
  • Demand planning and sales-to-operations alignment
  • Removal of non-performing layers, legacy processes and value leakage
03

Exit readiness, M&A & integration

Most value is won in the eighteen months before a process starts, not during it.

  • Equity story development and investor preparation
  • Due diligence readiness, data rooms and management information
  • Deal support: process management, negotiation support, integration planning
  • Post-acquisition synergy capture and operational alignment
  • Multiple expansion through recurring revenue and operational discipline

Case parallel

Food ingredients supplier — £10m turnover grown to £32m and sold to a listed Nordic food group at 11× earnings, with our founder leading finance throughout. Financial narrative shaped in-house; the owners supported through negotiation.

04

Governance, succession & non-executive

Family-owned businesses rarely fail for want of effort. They stall for want of structure.

  • Board structure, cadence and papers that make decisions happen
  • Succession planning and ownership transition
  • Risk, controls and delegated authority frameworks
  • Non-executive director and advisory board roles
05

Data & BI — Themis MIS

Reporting is the fifth mandate, not an add-on. Power BI and NetSuite Analytics Warehouse delivered by our own consultants.

  • BI strategy and reporting architecture aligned to how the business decides
  • Power BI end to end — data models, DAX and refresh pipelines, plus the interface on top
  • NetSuite Analytics Warehouse implementation and enrichment
  • Available full-time, fractionally or as a fixed-scope project
