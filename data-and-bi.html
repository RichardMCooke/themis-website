BI strategy & reporting optimisation

Committed to enhancing organisational decision-making through effective BI strategies and tools.

  • Reporting landscape review — what exists, what's trusted, what's quietly duplicated
  • A single definition per metric, agreed and documented, before anything is built
  • Reporting architecture and roadmap aligned to how the business actually decides
  • Optimisation of existing reporting systems — refresh times, licence cost, maintenance burden
  • Actionable analytics for the teams that use them, not just the board that commissions them

NetSuite Analytics Warehouse implementation

Getting NetSuite data out of NetSuite and into a warehouse the whole business can report from.

  • NSAW implementation, configuration and data enrichment
  • Mapping NetSuite subject areas to the metrics the business already uses
  • Blending NetSuite with non-NetSuite sources — CRM, WMS, spreadsheets that refuse to die

  • Governance, access and ongoing support model
