Who we are

This site is operated by Themis Professional Services Limited, a company registered in England & Wales (No. 06606104) with its registered office in Telford, Shropshire. Where this notice says "we", "us" or "the practice", it means that company, including the Themis MIS (Data & BI) division.

For everything covered by this notice we are the data controller, with the exceptions set out under Client data. We have not appointed a statutory Data Protection Officer — the practice is not required to — so the founder is personally accountable for data protection.

Data protection contact

Richard Cooke ACMA CGMA, Founder — richardcooke@themisprofessional.com · 01952 37 90 70. Put "Data protection" in the subject line and it comes to him directly.

What we collect

We collect personal data in four situations, and only what each one needs.

01

When you enquire

Your name, company, email address, telephone number, the division you selected and whatever you write in the message. The enquiry form on this site has no server behind it — submitting it opens your own email client and sends the message to us as ordinary email, so nothing is stored on this website at any point.

02

During an engagement

Contact and role details for the people we work with at a client — directors, finance staff, advisers and lenders — together with correspondence, meeting notes and engagement records. Where an engagement involves payroll, remuneration or personnel matters, we may see personal data about employees who are not our clients; that is covered under Client data.

03

When you visit this site

If — and only if — you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 records pages viewed, approximate location derived from a truncated IP address, device and browser type, and how you arrived. Your IP address is anonymised and we never see a name. Our host also keeps routine server logs for security and fault diagnosis.

04

When you apply to work with us

CVs, covering notes, references and interview notes sent to us by you or by a recruiter, whether or not we advertised a role.

We do not knowingly collect special category data — health, ethnicity, beliefs, biometrics — and we ask you not to send it. We do not collect data about children. This site is not intended for anyone under 18.

Client data — where we act as processor

This is the part that matters most in our line of work. When we act as a fractional CFO, build a reporting model or run a diligence exercise, we handle data belonging to the client: ledgers, payroll files, customer and supplier records, board papers, data warehouses.

  • In respect of that data the client is the controller and we act as processor, on written instructions, under the data-processing terms in the engagement letter.
  • We use client data only to deliver the engagement. We never use it for our own purposes, never combine it across clients, and never use it to train or feed any third-party model.
  • We prefer to work inside the client's own systems and permissions. Where we must hold a copy, it is kept only as long as the engagement requires and then returned or destroyed on instruction.
  • Data is anonymised or aggregated before it appears in any benchmark, case study or credential, and we seek the client's written approval before naming them.
  • Every person working on an engagement — director, consultant or specialist associate — is bound by confidentiality and by the same processing terms.

If you are an employee, customer or supplier of one of our clients and want to know what is held about you, ask that organisation — they are the controller. Tell us and we will pass the request to them promptly and help them answer it.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies at all until you choose to accept analytics. Consent Mode is set to deny by default, so on a first visit nothing is stored and nothing is sent to Google.

If you click Accept, Google Analytics 4 loads and sets its own cookies to distinguish one visit from another. IP anonymisation is on. Advertising storage, remarketing and personalisation signals are permanently off — we run no advertising from this site. Analytics data is retained by Google for 14 months and Google acts as our processor.

If you click Decline, no analytics script loads. Either way your choice is recorded in your browser's own local storage — not in a cookie, and never sent anywhere. To be asked again, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

The contact page embeds a Google Map. Loading it means Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies; that is the only third-party request on the site. Our address is also printed in plain text on that page if you would rather not load it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, ever, to anyone. We share it in four narrow circumstances: with service providers who run the tools we work in, with the client's own advisers where an engagement requires it and the client has asked us to, with our professional advisers and insurers where we need advice or must defend a claim, and with a regulator, court or law-enforcement body where we are legally obliged to.

The providers who may process personal data on our behalf are:

Microsoft

Email, files, Teams, Power BI. UK/EU data residency.

Google

Analytics and the embedded map, on consent only.

Website host

Serves these pages and keeps short-lived server logs.

Accountants & insurers

Statutory records and professional indemnity cover.

Each is bound by a written contract limiting them to our instructions. Where a provider transfers data outside the UK, that transfer relies on UK adequacy regulations or on the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We will confirm the position for any specific provider on request.

How we keep it safe

Client financial data is the most sensitive material we touch, so the controls are deliberately dull and consistently applied: multi-factor authentication on every account, encryption in transit and at rest, access granted per engagement on a need-to-know basis and withdrawn when it ends, no client data on personal or unmanaged devices, and no transfer of files over consumer messaging or personal email.

If a breach occurs that is likely to risk your rights, we will tell the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell you without undue delay. Where we are acting as processor, we notify the client controller immediately and support their response.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to do any of the following. Email the data protection contact above; we will acknowledge promptly and respond within one month, and it costs you nothing.

Access

A copy of what we hold about you.

Rectification

Correction of anything inaccurate or incomplete.

Erasure

Deletion, where no legal duty requires us to keep it.

Restriction

A pause on our use of it while a dispute is settled.

Objection

An end to processing we base on legitimate interests.

Portability

A machine-readable copy of what you gave us.

Withdraw consent

Turn analytics off by clearing this site's data.

Human decisions

Nothing about you is decided automatically here.

We may ask you to confirm your identity before we release information — a sensible precaution, not an obstacle. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive we may charge a reasonable fee or decline it, and we will explain why in writing.

Complaints, and changes to this notice

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first — most problems are a misunderstanding and we would rather fix it than defend it. If you are still not satisfied you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow SK9 5AF · 0303 123 1113 · ico.org.uk. Complaining to us first does not affect that right.
