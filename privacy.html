Privacy & cookies

How we handle information you give us, and what we measure when you visit this site.

Who we are

This site is operated by Themis Professional Services Limited, Company Reg. No. 06606104, registered in England & Wales, Telford, Shropshire. We are the data controller for information collected here. For any question about this notice, or to ask us to delete what we hold about you, email richardcooke@themisprofessional.com or call 01952 37 90 70.

Information you give us

If you use the enquiry form or email us directly, we receive the details you choose to send — typically your name, company, email address, telephone number and a description of what you need. We use it for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and, if it progresses, to discuss and deliver an engagement.

  • We do not sell, rent or share enquiry details with third parties for marketing
  • We do not add you to a mailing list without asking
  • Enquiries that do not progress are deleted within 24 months

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and how visitors arrive. It sets cookies in your browser and sends aggregated usage data to Google. We use it to improve the site — nothing more.

Nothing is set until you accept. Analytics cookies stay switched off until you click Accept on the notice at the bottom of the screen. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to Google. Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage; clear your site data to be asked again.

IP addresses are anonymised. Advertising, remarketing and personalisation signals are permanently switched off — we do not run advertising from this site.

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months. Google acts as our processor; details of its own handling are in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our processing it. Write to the address above and we will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Third-party content

The contact page embeds a Google Map. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive your IP address. If you would rather avoid that, our address is on the contact page in plain text.
